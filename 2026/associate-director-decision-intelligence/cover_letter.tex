build practical systems that remove friction without weakening quality. This is

analysis, coding, review, and documentation tasks into scalable team
capabilities.

Having led, coached, and mentored the 'aNCA' package team of 7 developers, I

I would like to explain why my current profile fits the \Position{} role. The

I am ready to apply this mix of technical depth, product ownership, AI
