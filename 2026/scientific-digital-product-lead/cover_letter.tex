\section{About myself}
As a Senior Data Scientist and Product Owner with 7+ years in regulated pharma
data science, my goal is to secure the \Position{} position. I specialize in
turning clinical and scientific workflows into reusable data products,
automated pipelines, and AI-enabled tools that scientists can trust in daily
work. I led the 'aNCA' open-source R package from concept to enterprise-wide

product ownership, and hands-on engineering to clinical informatics and digital
ecosystems.

\section{My mindset}
My working style is built around strategy, optimization, and automation. I like
to start from the real scientific workflow, understand where decisions slow
down, and then build practical systems that remove friction without weakening
quality. Having led, coached, and mentored the 'aNCA' package team of 7
developers, I have learned that technical impact only matters when users adopt
it. My strongest work combines hands-on engineering with product ownership,
stakeholder alignment, training, and clear measures of value.

I would like to explain why my current profile fits the \Position{} role. The
role sits where I have built my strongest impact: translating complex scientist
needs into roadmaps, aligning stakeholders, automating regulated workflows,
defining measurable value, and helping teams adopt new digital capabilities
without losing quality, traceability, or user trust.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Product strategy and adoption:} My work leading the 'aNCA'
        package shows that I can mentor 7 developers, manage a 15+ stakeholder
        network, set a clear roadmap, and deliver a product from concept to
        broad scientific adoption.
  \item \textbf{Clinical workflow and data products:} I have built end-to-end
        PK/PD and clinical data workflows, from raw clinical data to
        analysis-ready datasets, reporting, visualization, and submission

  \item \textbf{Technical execution with measurable value:} I automated ADPC,
        ADNCA, and ADPP dataset generation, reducing manual effort by 90\% and

  \item \textbf{AI-enabled delivery with control:} I scale multi-agent coding
        workflows for issue creation, problem solving, and code review while

\end{itemize}

I am ready to apply this mix of scientific context, product leadership,
AI-enabled automation, and regulated delivery to build digital products that are
