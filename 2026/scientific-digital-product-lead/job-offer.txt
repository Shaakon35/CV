The Position

A healthier future. It is what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance
science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for
generations to come. Creating a world where we all have more time with the
people we love. That is what makes us Roche.

Advances in AI, data, and computational sciences are transforming drug
discovery and development. Roche's Research and Early Development organisations
at Genentech (gRED) and Pharma (pRED) have demonstrated how these technologies
accelerate R&D, leveraging data and novel computational models to drive impact.
Seamless data sharing and access to models across gRED and pRED are essential
to maximising these opportunities. The new Computational Sciences Center of
Excellence (CS CoE) is a strategic, unified group whose goal is to harness this
transformative power of data and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to assist our
scientists in both pRED and gRED to deliver more innovative and transformative
medicines for patients worldwide.

The Data and Digital Catalysts (DDC) department within the CS CoE is a diverse,
curious and action-driven team at the intersection of science, engineering, and
computation and with ambition to advance our technical excellence. The team
focuses on partnering with scientific communities to build a robust
computational and data ecosystem that fuels scientific discovery and enables
faster, data-informed decision-making. We aim to modernize our ability to
acquire, store, link, share, find, and analyze data across the organization
through scalable, integrated, and forward-looking solutions that truly make
every data point count.

Reporting to the Domain Head of Translation & Development within DDC, the
Scientific Product Lead will play a pivotal role in fostering strong
partnerships with RED stakeholders, developing strategic vision, a deep
understanding of the "why," and prioritizing the "what" and "when" into an
actionable roadmap that delivers meaningful outcomes. The Product Lead will work
in close collaboration with DDC capability teams, shaping the "how" and aligning
the "who" to deliver technical excellence, ensuring the development of
integrated, innovative, and best-in-class products and platforms that accelerate
scientific discovery within the REDs.

The Opportunity

Join Roche's Translation & Development domain and drive the next generation of
Early Development Clinical Informatics. As Scientific Digital Product Lead, you
will bridge cutting-edge science and digital innovation - translating complex
clinical processes into scalable, automated, and AI-powered ecosystems. You will
act as the strategic engine behind our data infrastructure, building sustainable
pipelines that empower both scientists and advanced AI/ML research teams. If you
possess deep domain expertise in clinical development, proven digital product
leadership, and a passion for applying AI to accelerate therapeutic
breakthroughs, this is your chance to join the Translation & Development team.

Develop and refine the product family strategy within the Translation &
Development domain, ensuring alignment with key strategic initiatives. Enable
seamless integration of products (applications/platforms) into our evolving data
and digital ecosystem while contributing to the consolidation and deprecation of
legacy systems, resulting in a reduction of technical debt.

Demonstrate a deep understanding of the needs of scientists, applying strong
data and digital acumen to shape existing and the next generation of products.
Leverage this understanding to guide cross-functional teams, nurture stakeholder
partnerships, and manage their expectations, ensuring the execution of
initiatives that deliver tangible value to our scientists.

Collaborate with technical/engineering and data experts to guide and enable the
development of clinical workflows that incorporate scalable automation and
modular concepts, driving faster and higher-quality insights from translational
and clinical data.

Understand and translate the needs of scientists and the business into
actionable plans. Drive business analysis, demonstrate advanced product and
program/project management skills, prioritize tasks within a given framework,
effectively manage budgets, and oversee external workforce contributions.

Boldly advocate for future-facing solutions by anticipating emerging
capabilities and integrating them into strategic roadmaps. Identify key trends,
technologies, and methodologies to ensure innovation and long-term
competitiveness.

Build and cultivate trusted internal and external partnerships with
collaborators, fostering effective teamwork in a global setting.

Be willing to span the domain and contribute where needed, even outside of your
core area of expertise.

Who You Are

The ideal candidate will meet many of the following requirements:

You hold a Master's degree or PhD in life sciences, computer science, or a
related field, ideally coupled with an MBA or equivalent qualification. You
bring 5+ years of relevant professional experience in drug development, with a
strong emphasis on digital product ownership and strategy.

You have deep scientific knowledge in clinical processes and workflows, in
particular around clinical trial execution. Technical and engineering skills
(e.g., Python, vibe-coding workflow development) are a strong advantage.

You are a quick learner who excels at embracing new challenges and driving
scientific and computational innovation across complex scientific domains by
applying rapidly evolving new AI technologies.

You demonstrate a strong combination of strategic vision and operational
excellence, balancing long-term planning with hands-on execution to deliver
impactful outcomes. You excel in navigating highly ambiguous or evolving
situations with adaptability and focus.

You have a proven ability to lead large-scale, cross-functional projects
involving multiple teams and stakeholders. Experience in platform strategy,
advanced product management, business analysis, and change management is highly
valued, with prior work in a drug discovery setting strongly preferred.

You are an exceptional collaborator, capable of navigating complex
organizational landscapes, communicating clearly across diverse audiences, and
translating big-picture transformation strategies into actionable, incremental
steps that drive measurable progress.

You have a forward-thinking and agile mindset, with a passion for innovation and
curiosity. You are adept at integrating cutting-edge technologies and
methodologies into translation and development workflows, prioritizing value
creation, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
