\section{About myself}
As a Senior Data Scientist and Product Owner with 7+ years in regulated clinical
data science, my goal is to secure the \Position{} position. My experience is
centered on clinical data standards, CDISC SDTM/ADaM datasets, SAS/R clinical
programming, QC, GxP documentation, and late-phase eSubmission support. I have
worked on PK modeling package deliverables including ADPP, ADPC, PC, ADPPK, and
QC-T across multiple Roche disease areas, including Oncology and
Neuroscience/Rare Disease (NRD). I have also led the 'aNCA' open-source R
package from concept to enterprise-wide adoption by 120+ clinical and
pre-clinical scientists, delivering more than 2M CHF in annual savings.

\section{My mindset}
My working style is built around standards, automation, and practical adoption.
I like to understand how teams really prepare data, where compliance checks slow
down, and which reusable tools or guidance can make the process clearer. My best
work combines hands-on clinical data expertise with product ownership, training,
documentation, and measurable process improvement.

I would like to explain why my current profile fits the \Position{} role. The
role asks for someone who can connect regulatory requirements, clinical data
standards, data management, statistical programming, and study-team support. This
is close to the work I have done across Roche, Novartis, and Sanofi: building
submission-ready clinical datasets, preparing eSubmission package components,
documenting regulated workflows, and helping cross-functional teams understand
how data deliverables need to be prepared and checked.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Clinical data standards:} I create and QC SDTM, ADaM, NCA,
        PK/PD, ADPP, ADPC, PC, ADPPK, and QC-T outputs from raw clinical data.
  \item \textbf{Regulatory delivery:} My work includes electronic regulatory
        submission support for late-phase PK modeling packages, FDA/EMA query
        responses, GxP practices, documentation, code review, and
        quality-controlled datasets.
  \item \textbf{Process improvement:} I automated ADPC, ADNCA, and ADPP dataset
        generation by converting legacy SAS programs into reproducible R
        pipelines, reducing manual effort by 90\% and saving 30 data scientists
        about 70\% of their time on these tasks.
  \item \textbf{Leadership and enablement:} As Product Owner of 'aNCA', I led 7
        developers, managed a 15+ stakeholder network, onboarded users, and
        turned a manual workflow into regulated software used at scale.
  \item \textbf{Communication across functions:} I regularly work with clinical,
        data management, statistical programming, biostatistics, and
        pharmacometric stakeholders, translating technical requirements into
        clear deliverables, guidance, and reusable tools.
\end{itemize}

I am ready to apply this combination of clinical data standards, regulated
delivery, automation, and stakeholder leadership to help study teams produce
accurate, consistent, and compliant submission data packages.
