\section{About myself}
I am a Senior Data Scientist with 7+ years of pharmaceutical data science

and experimental data into decisions. I am applying for the \Position{} role

and drug-development impact.

\section{Why I fit the role}
The role calls for scientists who can frame difficult biological questions,
design experiments, write reusable code, evaluate results, communicate clearly,
and collaborate across a distributed research organization. My strongest fit is
on the applied scientific software and drug-development side:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Drug-development data science:} I have delivered PK/PD,
        pharmacometric, and clinical data workflows across Oncology and
        Neuroscience Phase 1 to 3 studies, partnering with clinical,
        statistical, and pharmacometric teams.
  \item \textbf{Reusable scientific code:} I founded and led aNCA, an
        open-source R package now used by 120+ clinical and pre-clinical
        scientists, establishing version control, testing, documentation, code
        review, CI/CD, and regulatory-grade development standards.
  \item \textbf{Machine learning and evaluation:} I have built and evaluated
        machine learning models including Logistic Regression, Gradient
        Boosting, and Random Forest, and used explainable-AI techniques such as
        feature importance and SHAP to make model behavior interpretable.
  \item \textbf{Experiment-to-impact workflow:} I design reproducible pipelines
        from data acquisition and preparation through analysis, review,
        reporting, and stakeholder decision making, reducing manual effort by
        90 percent in core workflows.
  \item \textbf{Scientific communication:} I present technical work externally
        at PHUSE and PAGE, translate scientific requirements into engineering
        plans, and lead cross-functional teams around shared analytical tools.
\end{itemize}

\section{What I would bring}
My background is strongest in applied pharmaceutical data science, scientific
software engineering, and practical machine learning rather than in theoretical
generative modeling research. That said, I bring a rare combination of domain
fluency, hands-on implementation, open-source product ownership, and the ability
to scale reproducible analytical workflows in a regulated scientific
environment. I would contribute immediately to experiment design, reusable code,
evaluation discipline, collaboration with scientists, and translation of machine
learning methods into tools that support medicine development.

I would welcome the chance to discuss where this applied ML and scientific
software profile could best support your Frontier Research agenda.
